# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or identity. Be considerate, assume good intent while addressing impact, give constructive feedback, and focus discussion on ideas and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and sharing another person's private information without permission. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Include relevant links or context when possible. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and respond with care.
